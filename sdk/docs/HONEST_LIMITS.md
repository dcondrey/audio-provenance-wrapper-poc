WHAT WILL NOT WORK. Written for the README and mirrored in the result types, so the API cannot outrun the engineering.

ACOUSTIC RE-RECORDING IS UNSUPPORTED BY DESIGN. Audio played through a speaker and captured by a microphone returns `not_found`. This is the headline capability in the brief and it is not delivered. The failure is structural, not a tuning deficit: room reverberation is a dense multi-tap convolution that acts as a systematic, content-correlated bias on the exact log-spectral-energy differences the mark modulates. Repetition, interleaving and block combining all average out INDEPENDENT noise; the room is time-invariant, so the same pairs are corrupted in every block and the errors are correlated rather than averaging. Both judge panels scored this 2 and 3.5 out of 10 and both called it structural. The published comparables agree: the closest apples-to-apples scheme scores 56% bit accuracy at 5 cm (chance) while acing every electronic channel, and the strongest published non-neural method reaches only 66% at 1 m, below the capacity any usable code rate needs. `capabilities().acousticRerecording` therefore returns the literal string `"unsupported"`, never null, because null reads as "not yet measured" and invites hope. Do not describe GenoMark as surviving playback in a room, a phone recording, or over-the-air capture. The only path that works is a trained encoder/decoder with a differentiable room-impulse-response distortion layer, which is out of scope for a pure-TypeScript SDK; the payload's version and namespace fields exist so such a detector can be added later behind the same registry key.

THERE IS NO ACOUSTIC PROFILE. The candidate design's Profile A is cut, not shipped-disabled. It was audible (predicted ODG -1.5 to -2.5, in the band where vocals live), required 37.5 s of continuous uninterrupted near-field capture, and had NEGATIVE capacity margin at 1 m. Shipping it behind an opt-in named "the acoustic profile" would generate exactly the support burden its own analysis predicts.

LOWPASS AND NARROWBAND PROCESSING ARE FATAL, AND THIS IS A REGRESSION VERSUS THE REJECTED DESIGN. The mark lives in 861-4307 Hz. That ceiling is deliberate and is why this design survives HE-AAC's SBR crossover where a 2-7 kHz design does not. The same choice makes it strictly worse under lowpass: a 4 kHz lowpass removes nearly the entire band (the rejected design would have kept half its groups), and any telephony-band path at 3.4 kHz destroys the payload completely. Both directions of this tradeoff belong in the docs; publishing only the SBR win would be selective.

PARAMETRIC STEREO AND VERY LOW BITRATES DEGRADE IT. At 32 kbps HE-AAC v2 / xHE-AAC the SBR crossover falls near the band top so the mark largely survives the cut, but Parametric Stereo collapses the mid channel the mark lives in. Predicted ~70% recovery, unmeasured. The supported compression envelope is MP3 >= 128 kbps, AAC-LC >= 96 kbps, HE-AAC v1 >= 48 kbps, Opus >= 64 kbps.

AGGRESSIVE MASTERING CAN STILL REMOVE IT. Pair differencing over 86 Hz cancels any response smooth over that span, and a static guard mask keeps pairs off the common multiband crossovers at 1000, 1200, 2000, 2500, 3000 and 3500 Hz. Neither handles a crossover the mask does not know about, nor fast per-band gain riding, and neither is any defence at all against an equaliser shaped like the published cell layout rather than smooth across it: pair differencing cancels a response that is FLAT over 86 Hz, and the removal attacks measured in `docs/WATERMARK_ATTACKS.md` are deliberately not flat over 86 Hz. Streaming normalization to -14 LUFS with a multiband limiter, broadcast chains, and phone-recorder AGC are the residual hazard, predicted ~90% and on the DEFAULT distribution path rather than an edge case.

SHORT AUDIO RETURNS NOTHING. Under 9.66 s of contiguous marked audio yields no GenoMark decode at all, and that figure assumes the file happens to start on a block boundary. An arbitrary crop needs 19.32 s, and the higher-confidence `strong` class needs 19.32 s typical and 28.98 s guaranteed. A track assembled from sub-block fragments returns `not_found`, not a partial answer.

SPEED AND PITCH MANIPULATION, WITH THE THRESHOLDS NOW MEASURED. Playback rate changes beyond the searched grid fall outside it. Pitch-preserving time-stretch and independent pitch shift break the single-rate model entirely, because time and frequency axes move by different factors; these are expected failures, not bugs. A 2-D search would cost a 16x false-positive multiplier and a wider CRC and is deliberately unimplemented. HOW MUCH IS NEEDED, over six real recordings: a pitch-preserving tempo change costs nothing at 0.6%, takes half the corpus at 0.75% and all of it at 1%; a tempo-preserving pitch shift costs nothing at 3 cents, takes a third at 5 cents and all of it at 10 cents, which is a tenth of a semitone and one ffmpeg filter. Coupled playback-rate change is the most robust axis and did not go to zero anywhere in the searched range: nothing at 0.2%, one item at 0.3%, half at 0.75%. Deleting one sample in a thousand cost nothing on 6 of 6, and neither did random +-0.2% piecewise warping over 2 s segments. State the pitch-shift figure to anyone who asks what it takes to strip the mark; it is the cheapest attack there is.

SPARSE TONAL CONTENT IS WEAK IN BOTH DIRECTIONS AT ONCE. Solo piano, harpsichord, clean electric guitar, unaccompanied voice and near-silence have few pairs carrying energy, so slots puncture and blocks fail to close. The same material is where the mark is most likely to be audible. Robustness and fidelity degrade together on this class rather than trading off.

THE MARK IS REMOVABLE BY ANYONE WHO READS THE SPEC, KEYED OR NOT, AND THIS IS MEASURED. In namespace 0 the profile key is a published constant. That was already stated. What was NOT stated, and is now measured over six real recordings in `docs/WATERMARK_ATTACKS.md`, is that a KEYED mark is no better off against an attacker who has read `GENOMARK_SPEC.md`. The dither is secret; the band geometry and the lattice period are not, and removal only needs the geometry. Five independent spec-only methods erased the payload on 6 of 6 items: a fixed +-1.95 dB equaliser of the published cell shape applied open loop with no analysis of the file at all, a half-period offset of the slot statistic, a full-period random offset, a five-slot median flattening, and simply running the published embedder under a key of the attacker's own. Two of those cost LESS than the mark itself: paired per item, embedding cost 29.5 to 52.5 dB segmental SNR and randomising the coset removed it at 29.9 to 52.0 dB, within 0.5 dB on every item, while median flattening removed it at a residual smaller than the mark on all six. An attack that removes the mark while destroying the audio is vandalism; these are not that. The public namespace provides provenance RECOVERY for cooperative and accidental cases, a keyed namespace resists a CASUAL remover and nothing more, and neither is tamper resistance. Mid/side manipulation weakens the mark proportionally and an S-only extraction removes it entirely.

THE MARK CAN BE FORGED WITHOUT THE KEY, AND FOR A REGISTERED RELEASE THE ATTACKER NEEDS ONLY THE FILE AND THE REGISTRY. This is the most serious finding in `docs/WATERMARK_ATTACKS.md` and it was not previously stated anywhere. The detector reads one quantity per slot: which coset of the DELTA lattice the statistic sits on. Every block of a key epoch repeats the same dither and the same coded word, so those cosets are directly readable off a marked file with no key. Measured: their mean resultant length is 0.9965 to 0.9998 on marked audio against 0.4545 to 0.5475 on the same audio unmarked. Consequences, all measured at 6 of 6 with detector confidence class `strong`. (1) TRANSPLANT: read the cosets off one marked donor and write them onto unrelated audio, and that unrelated audio now decodes to the donor's locator. 8.87 s of donor at 48 kHz is enough, which is one block. (2) CHOSEN-PAYLOAD FORGERY: the convolutional code, the interleaver and the slot roles are published, so an attacker who also knows the payload the donor carries can subtract the known coded bit from the observed coset, recover the key-derived dither of that epoch, and write ANY payload of their choosing under the victim's secret key. A registered work's locator is registry data, so for a published release that knowledge is free. The 32-bit CRC is not what fails here and it is doing its job: 0 accepts in 300 random-coset trials, against an arithmetic bound near 6e-8 per detection. A CRC stops a guess; it does not stop a copy. Do not describe a GenoMark detection as evidence that a file came from the party the locator names.

COLLUSION BREAKS PER-RECIPIENT MARKING, AND CHEAPLY. Several differently marked copies of the SAME recording under one key can be averaged. Measured on one recording: two copies survive, three copies make the detector return a DIFFERENT colluder's locator at class `strong`, and five or more erase the payload entirely at a segmental SNR of 79 dB, about 41 dB quieter than the mark itself. Averaging near-identical masters is perceptually almost free, which is what makes this matter more than the count suggests. Audio Provenance does not currently claim a per-recipient forensic use, and this is why that claim must not be made without a design that addresses collusion.

WHAT A DETECTION ACTUALLY MEANS. A recovered payload is a SOFT binding. It resolves a registry key; it is not evidence the audio is unmodified, which is what the hash-chain hard binding decides after the signature verifies. Given the transplant result above it is not evidence of origin either: a CRC-valid payload proves only that SOMETHING wrote that coset pattern into this audio, and anyone holding a marked file can write it. The mark's honest job is to find the record; the signature and the hash chain decide what is true, and the signed reference constellation is the second, independent question that a transplant cannot answer. A single-block detection is proof level `inferred` at best and must never be reported as `verified` on its own. `result.match === 1.0` if and only if a hard binding matched exactly; soft bases cap at 0.99.

IDENTITY IS USUALLY NULL, AND THAT IS CORRECT. `result.identity` is non-null if and only if `identityProofLevel === "externally_verified"`. A valid self-generated signature proves KEY POSSESSION and says nothing about who holds the key, so the common case for an unanchored signer is `identity: null` with status `untrusted`, even though the signature is cryptographically perfect. This is the prior art's central invariant and the reason the field is nullable in the type system.

AN ATTACKER CAN INFLICT `untrusted`. Appending a broken manifest to someone's file yields `untrusted`, because a signature-invalid embedded manifest is a positive finding that a lower ladder rung may not launder away. That is a reputation nuisance, not a false accept, and the file genuinely does contain a broken manifest. Both the broken candidate and any lower-rung finding appear in `result.recovery`.

`not_found` NEVER MEANS "NO MANIFEST EXISTS". It means none was recovered. `recovery.exhausted` and `result.incomplete` say how hard we looked. A registry outage sets `incomplete` and never becomes a verdict.

THE CORE IS PURE TYPESCRIPT; THE FORMAT SUPPORT IS NOT. The DSP core takes `{ pcm, sampleRate }` with no native dependency, and WAV parsing ships in-package. MP3, AAC, FLAC and container demuxing require an explicit DecoderPort (an ffmpeg child process on Node, decodeAudioData in a browser). Any claim that the package as a whole is dependency-free would be false.

EVERY PERFORMANCE FIGURE ABOVE IS A PREDICTION. No bench has run. The ODG values are model output, not measurement; `measuredTransparency` and `measuredSurvival` are null until a campaign fills them. The chips-per-slot budget rests on a noise constant (sigma_d) that is measured in build unit C0 BEFORE the embedder is written, precisely because the same criticism sank the rejected design's rate budget. Nothing here may be stated as measured performance until the bench says so, and soft-binding `verified` is blocked at the type level until the null test produces a real false-positive rate.
